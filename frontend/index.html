ABOUT PROJECT

Smart Technology for Electric Mobility

EV SmartCharge is a web-based smart electric vehicle charging platform designed to help EV users understand battery requirements, charging costs and charging station information.

The project also demonstrates modern DevOps practices including Git, GitHub, Docker and Jenkins CI/CD.
